four PDFs.

authority and the property of their authors. If you are an author or rights holder and would like
something credited differently or removed, please open an issue on the repository.

## The course

- **Cors da dialett da Rögn**, the weekly dialect course in Arogno (2023 onwards), taught by
  **Guido Casellini**, who wrote the lessons, the grammar sheets (conditional, subjunctive, imperative,
  gerund) and the exercise sheets, and translated the Italian exercises, dialogues and stories into
  dialect.
- The other members of the class, whose answers and sentences appear in several of the exercise sheets.

## Reference material consulted

Used to check general Western Lombard verb patterns. None of it is specific to Arogno, and no text was
copied from it.

| Source | What it was used for |
|---|---|
| [Milanese verb conjugation (Verbix)](https://docs.verbix.com/Languages/Milanese) | The four conjugation classes; present, imperfect and future endings to compare with the course |
| [Lombard/Morphology (Wikibooks)](https://en.wikibooks.org/wiki/Lombard/Morphology) | Overview of the verb system |
| [Lombard/Irregular verbs (Wikibooks)](https://en.wikibooks.org/wiki/Lombard/Irregular_verbs) | Forms and participles of *volé*, *tegnì*, *vegnì*; the list of irregular verbs |
| [Lombard/Modal verbs (Wikibooks)](https://en.wikibooks.org/wiki/Lombard/Modal_verbs) | The note that *dové* is rare and often replaced by an "avere da" construction |
| [Lombard/Past Indicative (Wikibooks)](https://en.wikibooks.org/wiki/Lombard/Past_Indicative) | Which verbs take which auxiliary |
| [Lombard/Present Subjunctive (Wikibooks)](https://en.wikibooks.org/wiki/Lombard/Present_Subjunctive) | Example verbs for each class |
| [fà (Wiktionary)](https://www.wiktionary.com/wiki/f%C3%A0) and [savé (Wiktionary)](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/savé) | Western Lombard forms of two irregular verbs |
| [Lombard (Wikibooks)](https://en.wikibooks.org/wiki/Category:Book:Lombard) | A checklist of the topics a learner's grammar of Lombard covers, for the outline of `Grammar/`; no text was copied (the book describes Milanese, under CC BY-SA) |
| [Dialetto ticinese (Wikipedia, Italian)](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dialetto_ticinese) | Background on the varieties and the Ticinese koinè; has no conjugation tables |
| [Dialet ticines (Wikipedia, Lombard)](https://lmo.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dialet_ticines) | Background; has no conjugation tables |

The standard references for Ticinese, the *Lessico dialettale della Svizzera italiana* (CDE) and
Petrini's *La koinè ticinese* (1988), are not available online and were **not** consulted. They would be
the right place to verify the forms marked †.

## What was generated, and by whom

- The extraction scripts, page layout and PDFs were written with
  [Claude Code](https://claude.com/claude-code) (Anthropic).
- **English translations** were written by Claude from the Italian, not taken from the course.
- **Verb forms and infinitives marked †** were inferred by Claude from the patterns in the lessons. They
  are not the teacher's and may be wrong; see [TO-CHECK.md](TO-CHECK.md).
- **Entries marked ⚠** were paired automatically and may be misaligned.
- The dialect phrases on the dictionary and verb PDF covers were composed for the covers and are not from the
  course. The two sayings on the expressions cover are from the course (*15a leziun - proverbi* and *frasi
  fatte*).
- The **grammar** (`Grammar/`, `etc/grammar/`) was drafted by Claude, Italian and English, from the course
  material; its examples are all quoted from the course documents, with the line they come from. The
  glosses under the examples, and the rules marked †, are Claude's analysis. The sentence on the grammar
  cover is from *dialoghi a Lugano*; its dedication, *Mit Liebe, für Peter*, is to the classmate who asked
  for the grammar. None of it has been checked by the teacher yet.
- The **topics** the expressions are grouped by, and the choice of which sayings to move out of the
  dictionary, are Claude's.

Whatever went wrong in the extraction, the translations or the inferred forms, the errors are mine.

## Software

Python with [openpyxl](https://openpyxl.readthedocs.io/); the CLDF tools [pycldf](https://github.com/cldf/pycldf) and [cldfbench](https://github.com/cldf/cldfbench), which build and validate the data set in `cldf/` according to the [CLDF specification](https://cldf.clld.org/); [Poppler](https://poppler.freedesktop.org/)
for PDF text; [Tesseract OCR](https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tesseract) with its Italian and German
models for the scanned documents; the Italian Hunspell word list for telling Italian from dialect; Google
Chrome for printing the PDFs; and the DejaVu fonts.
