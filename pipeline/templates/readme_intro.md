# Ticinese dialect: *dialett da Rögn*

Notes, exercises and reference pages from a weekly course in the Lombard dialect of Ticino, as spoken in
Arogno (*Rögn*), in the Lugano district of Italian-speaking Switzerland. The course is *Cors da dialett da
Rögn*, taught by Guido Casellini; the documents here were handed out between 2023 and 2026.

Dialect changes noticeably from one valley to the next, so this collection treats the Rögn course and the
teacher's own exercise sheets as the reference. Texts from other parts of Ticino (Mendrisiotto, Basso
Ceresio, Bellinzonese and others) are kept, but listed separately and labelled with where they come from.

The pages are written for an English speaker who reads Italian: each entry gives the Italian, the dialect
and, where available, the English.
